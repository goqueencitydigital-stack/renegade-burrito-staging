Fundraising: eat bold, do good

Renegade Fundraising Days help local organizations raise money while their community enjoys burritos, bowls and tacos.

At Renegade Burrito, we believe great food and great communities go hand in hand. We're proud to give back to the neighborhoods that support us.

Who can apply

Organizations must provide a tax-exempt letter and fall into one of these categories:

  • K-12 schools
  • Athletic boosters
  • Churches
  • Libraries
  • Youth groups
  • Universities and colleges
  • Animal organizations
  • Environmental organizations
