Mexican food in Arvada

Fast-casual Mexican food in Arvada at 88th and Wadsworth: burritos, street tacos, bowls, nachos, margaritas and a patio.

Order onlineArvada location

Looking for Mexican food in Arvada? Renegade Burrito at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd makes its salsas, proteins and pork green chili from scratch, and serves burritos, street tacos, bowls, quesadillas and nachos every day.

Arvada is the Renegade with the patio, margaritas and beer, and happy hour every day from 2 to 5 pm. The patio hosts private events for up to 40 guests.
