Food near Red Rocks Amphitheatre

Grab a burrito after the show — served fresh at the Red Rocks exits every Sunday.

Your post-show fuel

The lights fade, the crowd cheers, and your stomach suddenly remembers it's been dancing for hours. Renegade Burrito is waiting at the three main exits of Red Rocks every Sunday, serving warm, handheld food to end your night on a high note.

Heading to a show any other night? Our closest restaurant is Lakewood on Union Blvd — order ahead for pickup on the way.

Colorado-made burritos, tacos and bowls since 2017. Breakfast burritos served all day at six metro Denver restaurants.
