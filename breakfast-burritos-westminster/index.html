Breakfast burritos in Westminster

Breakfast burritos all day at the original Renegade, by Orchard Town Center at 136th and I-25. Order pickup or delivery.

Order onlineWestminster North location

Renegade Burrito started in Westminster in 2017, and breakfast burritos have been on the menu all day since. Our Westminster North restaurant at 13648 Orchard Parkway is near Orchard Town Center at 136th and I-25.

Order The Inferno, The Mile High or T's Gunslinger, or build your own with bacon, chorizo or sausage, eggs, seasoned potatoes and cheddar/jack.

Feeding a team in north Westminster or Broomfield? Call the Westminster restaurant for catering, or request a quote online.
