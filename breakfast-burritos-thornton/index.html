Breakfast burritos in Thornton

Breakfast burritos all day on Grant St in Thornton, near Northglenn Marketplace. Signature breakfast burritos and build-your-own. Order online.

Order breakfastThornton location

Renegade Burrito Thornton at 10005 Grant St, Unit E opens at 7 am every day and serves breakfast burritos until close — handy whether you're heading south on I-25 or grabbing lunch near Northglenn Marketplace.

Thornton's breakfast menu includes the Inferno, the Mile High and T's Gunslinger, plus build-your-own breakfast burritos, bowls, tacos and quesadillas.
