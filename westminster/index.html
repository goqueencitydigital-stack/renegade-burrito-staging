About this location

Renegade Burrito started right here in Westminster in 2017. Our Westminster North restaurant at 13648 Orchard Parkway is close to Orchard Town Center, Topgolf Thornton and the Denver Premium Outlets, with outdoor seating and online ordering. For catering from this location, call the store directly.
