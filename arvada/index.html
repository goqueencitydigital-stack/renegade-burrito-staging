About this location

Our Arvada restaurant sits at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd near Olde Town Arvada, Lake Arbor and Indian Tree Golf Club. It is the only Renegade that pours margaritas and beer, and it runs Happy Hour every day from 2 to 5 pm. The patio seats up to 40 guests for private events, with no reservation fee — just a minimum spend based on day and time.

  • Heated patio (seats up to 40 for private events)
  • Happy Hour 2–5 pm daily (Arvada only)
  • Margaritas and beer

Nearby: Near Olde Town Arvada, Lake Arbor and Indian Tree Golf Club, at 88th and Wadsworth.

Arvada/Westminster FAQs

Does Renegade Burrito Arvada have a patio?

Yes. Arvada has a patio that can host private events of up to 40 guests. Private event details.

When is happy hour in Arvada?

Every day from 2 to 5 pm. Happy hour is offered at Arvada only.

Can I order Renegade Burrito Arvada online?

Yes. Order pickup or delivery online from this page. Delivery depends on your address being in range.

Does Arvada serve alcohol?

Yes. Arvada serves margaritas and beer.

What are the hours at Arvada/Westminster?

Mon–Thu 7 am – 8 pm · Fri–Sat 7 am – 9 pm · Sun 7 am – 8 pm.
