Breakfast burritos in Denver

Breakfast burritos all day from two Denver kitchens: Denver – DU near Washington Park and Denver – Park Hill on E 39th Ave.

Order onlineDenver – DU location

Denver has two Renegades. Denver – DU at 2171 S Grape St is a pickup-and-delivery kitchen near the University of Denver, Washington Park and Cherry Creek. Denver – Park Hill at 5155 E 39th Ave serves carry-out and delivery.

Both serve breakfast burritos from open to close. Order on Toast, choose pickup or delivery at checkout, and grab it on the way to class, work or the park.

Park Hill's Toast menu also has Taco Tuesday street tacos, and both kitchens take catering orders.

Where is Renegade Burrito in Denver?

Is breakfast served all day?

Yes, every Renegade serves breakfast burritos from open to close.

Yes. Order on Toast for pickup or delivery. Delivery depends on your address.
