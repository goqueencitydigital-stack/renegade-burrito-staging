How to order catering

Box lunches, catering bars and party add-ons. Call the catering line or request a quote, and the restaurant confirms menu, price and timing.

Request a catering quoteCall to order (720) 469-9755
Notice: 4 hours preferred for box lunches and 12 packs, 48 hours for catering bars, on the current catering menu. Bigger events: request a quote.

Catering is ordered by phone or with a quote request. It can't be checked out online. Call the catering line or your nearest Renegade, or send your date, headcount and address and the restaurant will confirm menu, price and timing.
