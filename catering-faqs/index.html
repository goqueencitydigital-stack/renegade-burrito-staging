Catering questions

What types of events does Renegade Burrito cater?

We cater all types of events, from weddings to birthday parties, corporate events to smaller gatherings. We can accommodate as few as 20 people and up to 1,000 people. Box lunches start at 10.

Can catering accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or dairy-free needs?

We can accommodate many dietary restrictions, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and dairy-free. There is always a risk of cross-contamination, but we do our best to prevent it.

What catering options are there for breakfast, lunch or dinner?

Pre-made burritos are a great option for feeding a large group on the go. Box lunches include a regular-size burrito, chips, salsa and a cookie. Catering bars are a self-serve option with more variety and can be customized with add-ons like street tacos and nacho bars.

What areas do you cater?

Metro Denver and most of the Front Range, from Boulder to Fort Lupton and from the Denver Tech Center to Golden.

Do you deliver catering?

Yes. Delivery fees vary by distance. In-house delivery is available on catering orders over $150.

How do I place a catering order?

Order catering online on your location's Toast page, request a quote here, or call or email us at (720) 469-9755 / renegadeburrito@outlook.com.

How much notice do you need?

The current catering menu lists 4 hours' notice preferred for box lunches and 12 packs, and 48 hours for catering bars. More notice is better for large events.

Can I host a private event at a Renegade Burrito?

Yes. Our Arvada patio hosts up to 40 guests with no rental fee, just a minimum spend. Arvada private events.
