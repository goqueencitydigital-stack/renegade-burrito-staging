Mexican food in Thornton

Fast-casual Mexican food on Grant St in Thornton: burritos, street tacos, bowls, nachos and catering. Order online.

Order onlineThornton location

Renegade Burrito Thornton at 10005 Grant St, Unit E serves scratch-made Mexican food minutes from Northglenn Marketplace and Carpenter Park.

The menu runs from Customer Favorite burritos like Lenny's Outlaw and the Double R to street tacos, bowls, quesadillas and deluxe nachos built to share. Breakfast burritos are served all day.

Thornton offers catering box lunches, catering bars, chips and dips, cookies and corn ribs, ordered by phone or quote request. Current online offers are on the specials page.
