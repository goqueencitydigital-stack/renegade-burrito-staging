Frequently asked questions

What is the Inferno?

1/2 Bacon and 1/2 Chorizo with Potatoes, Eggs, Cheddar Jack Cheese, Renegade Hot sauce, Roasted Jalapeno Pico on a Jalapeno Cheddar Tortilla.

Which burritos use pork green chili?

Brad's Renegade is built on pork green chili, and The Desperado is half pork chili verde, half carnitas.
