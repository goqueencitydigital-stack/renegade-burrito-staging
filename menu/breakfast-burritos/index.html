Frequently asked questions

Do you serve breakfast all day?

Yes. Breakfast burritos, bowls, tacos and quesadillas are on the menu from open to close at every location.

What's in a Renegade breakfast burrito?

Seasoned potatoes, scrambled eggs, melted cheddar/jack cheese and your choice of salsa in a tortilla. Add bacon, chorizo or sausage, or pick a signature build like the Inferno or Mile High.

Allergen notice: our kitchens handle wheat, dairy, eggs, soy and other allergens. Gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan requests can often be accommodated, but cross-contact is possible. Nutrition information is not currently published. Menu and prices from each location's Toast online menu as of October 26, 2026.

Colorado-made burritos, tacos and bowls since 2017. Breakfast burritos served all day at six metro Denver restaurants and Section 127 at Empower Field.
