The best burrito near Broomfield is straight down Wadsworth

The closest Renegade burrito to Broomfield

Broomfield doesn't have a Renegade Burrito of its own yet, but one is a straight shot away. Renegade Burrito Arvada/Westminster at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B sits on Wadsworth Blvd at 88th Ave. Wadsworth, also known as State Highway 121, runs from the US 36 and US 287 interchange in Broomfield south through Westminster and Arvada, so you can stay on one road most of the way.

The Wadsworth store is the Renegade with the patio, margaritas and beer, and happy hour every day from 2 to 5 pm. Dine in, sit outside, order pickup or get delivery online if your address is in range.

Renegade Burrito started in nearby Westminster in 2017 and now has six metro Denver restaurants. The menu is built around Customer Favorite burritos, build-your-own burritos and bowls, street tacos and a lineup of house-made salsas.

Customer Favorites worth the drive from Broomfield

The Customer Favorites are Renegade's signature builds, and most come on a jalapeño cheddar tortilla. These are the ones on the Arvada/Westminster online menu, with the price listed there.

Getting here from Broomfield

Wadsworth Parkway is the easiest route from most of Broomfield:

  • From the US 36 / Wadsworth interchange: take Wadsworth Parkway south, through Westminster, to 88th Ave. The restaurant is at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B.
  • From Interlocken or FlatIron Crossing: take US 36 to the Wadsworth Parkway exit and head south.
  • From the 1stBank Center area: the arena is just east of Wadsworth Parkway on Arista Place. Get back on Wadsworth and head south.
  • From east Broomfield near I-25: our Westminster North store at 13648 Orchard Parkway, at 136th and I-25, may be closer. Westminster North details.

What makes a Renegade burrito

Built on a steamed tortilla

Every burrito starts on a freshly steamed tortilla with melted cheddar or jack cheese. Most Customer Favorites use the jalapeño cheddar tortilla.

Proteins with personality

Choose from Renegade chicken, chipotle grilled chicken, steak guisado, carnitas, pork chili verde, bacon, chorizo or sausage.

Potatoes or rice

Many Renegade burritos use seasoned potatoes instead of rice. You can pick rice, beans or seasoned potatoes when you build your own.

A salsa for every heat level

Pico, roasted jalapeño pico, mango pico, strawberry habanero, tomatillo ranch, salsa verde, roja and Renegade hot sauce. See the salsa guide.

Build your own

The build-your-own burrito is listed at $8.29 on the Arvada/Westminster online menu. Choose your protein; rice, beans or seasoned potatoes; pinto or black beans; fresh toppings like lettuce, onions, cilantro, sour cream and guacamole; and a salsa. The same build is available as a bowl or a quesadilla. Final options and upcharges always show online. Build-your-own menu.

Happy hour and the patio

If you're making the trip from Broomfield, make it count. The Arvada/Westminster store runs happy hour every day from 2 to 5 pm and is the only Renegade serving margaritas and beer. Happy hour details.

The patio hosts private events for up to 40 guests with no reservation fee, just a minimum spend based on the day and time. Plan a patio event.

Catering for Broomfield offices and teams

Broomfield businesses and teams can cater from the Arvada/Westminster store. Renegade catering is ordered by phone or with a quote request, not through online checkout. At Arvada/Westminster, the online menu lists box lunches at $13.99 each (any regular burrito, chips and salsa and a fresh-baked chocolate chip cookie), a build-your-own burrito catering bar at $14.99 per person for groups of more than 20, and a street taco catering bar at $13.99 per person.

Box lunches have a 10-box minimum. The current catering menu asks for about 4 hours' notice on box lunches and 48 hours on catering bars, and in-house delivery is available on catering orders over $150. Call the restaurant at (303) 993-4171, call the main catering line at (720) 469-9755, or send a catering quote request with your date, headcount and address.

Delivery fees vary by distance, so mention your Broomfield address when you call or request a quote.

Rewards for regulars

Every online order can earn Burrito Bucks, Renegade's rewards program: 1 point for every $1, $10 off for every 100 points, and 25 points just for signing up. Join Burrito Bucks before your first order so the points land on it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Renegade Burrito in Broomfield?

Not yet. The closest Renegade for most of Broomfield is Arvada/Westminster at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B, straight down Wadsworth Parkway at 88th Ave. East Broomfield near I-25 may be closer to Westminster North at 13648 Orchard Parkway.

How do I get to Renegade from Broomfield?

Take Wadsworth Parkway (State Highway 121) south from the US 36 interchange in Broomfield, through Westminster, to 88th Ave.

What are the best burritos at Renegade?

Renegade's signature Customer Favorites include Lenny's Outlaw, the Desperado, Lola G's Bandit, Big Al's Bonanza, Brad's Renegade, the Double R and the Spacebird.

Does Renegade deliver to Broomfield?

Delivery is chosen at checkout and depends on your address being in range. Enter your Broomfield address online to see if delivery is available.

How much are burritos?

On the Oct 26, 2026 Arvada/Westminster online menu, the build-your-own burrito was $8.29 and Customer Favorites ranged from $9.99 to $14.29.

What are the hours?

The current listing shows Mon–Thu 7 am – 8 pm · Fri–Sat 7 am – 9 pm · Sun 7 am – 8 pm. Check online ordering for holiday hours.

Can Renegade cater an event in Broomfield?

Yes. Call (303) 993-4171 or the catering line at (720) 469-9755, or send a quote request. Delivery fees depend on distance.

Does the Arvada/Westminster store serve drinks?

Yes. It's the only Renegade that serves margaritas and beer, with happy hour every day from 2 to 5 pm.
