Breakfast burritos for Broomfield, all day long

The closest Renegade breakfast burrito for most of Broomfield is at Renegade Burrito Arvada/Westminster, 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B, on Wadsworth Blvd at 88th Ave. Wadsworth Parkway (State Highway 121) connects Broomfield's US 36 interchange to the restaurant, so it's an easy morning run.

The store opens at 7 am every day and serves breakfast until close. Order ahead on Toast and your breakfast burrito is ready when you pull in, or get delivery if your address is in range.

Every Renegade breakfast burrito starts with scrambled eggs, seasoned potatoes and melted cheddar/jack cheese in a steamed tortilla. Add bacon, chorizo or sausage and a house salsa, or choose one of three breakfast signatures.

The breakfast lineup

All three breakfast signatures come on Renegade's jalapeño cheddar tortilla. Here's what's in each one and the price on the Arvada/Westminster Toast menu.

For a first visit, the Inferno is the classic: half bacon, half chorizo, Renegade hot sauce and roasted jalapeño pico. The Mile High is the pick for green chile fans, with pork green chili folded right in.

Your Broomfield breakfast route

  • From US 36 at Wadsworth: head south on Wadsworth Parkway through Westminster to 88th Ave.
  • From Interlocken or FlatIron Crossing: take US 36 to the Wadsworth Parkway exit and head south.
  • From the 1stBank Center area: the arena sits just east of Wadsworth Parkway on Arista Place, so you're already on the right road.
  • From east Broomfield near I-25: Renegade Westminster North at 13648 Orchard Parkway, at 136th and I-25, may be the closer stop. Westminster North details.

Ordering ahead on Toast is the fastest way to do a breakfast run before work.

What goes into a Renegade breakfast burrito

Eggs, potatoes and cheese

The base is the same every time: scrambled eggs, seasoned potatoes and melted cheddar/jack. It's what makes the burrito filling without rice.

Bacon, chorizo or sausage

Pick one when you build your own, or let a signature decide. The Inferno uses both bacon and chorizo, and T's Gunslinger is all chorizo.

Pork green chili

The Mile High adds Renegade's pork green chili, the same chili that shows up in Brad's Renegade and the Desperado.

The finishing salsa

Choose from mild pico, roasted jalapeño pico, salsa verde, mango pico, strawberry habanero or Renegade hot sauce. Salsa guide.

Breakfast bowls, tacos and quesadillas

  • Breakfast bowl: Seasoned Potatoes, Melted Cheddar/Jack Cheese, Scrambled Eggs, and your choice of freshly made Salsa in a Bowl. ($8.29)
  • Breakfast tacos: Seasoned Potatoes, Melted Cheddar/Jack Cheese, Scrambled Eggs, and your choice of fresh-made Salsa served in 3 Corn Taco Shells ($9.29)
  • Breakfast quesadilla: Seasoned Potatoes, Melted Cheddar/Jack Cheese, Scrambled Eggs, your choice of Salsa folded in a Tortilla fresh off the grill! ($9.29)

Breakfast for Broomfield offices

Breakfast meetings in Interlocken or anywhere in Broomfield can be catered from the Arvada/Westminster store. Renegade catering is ordered by phone or with a quote request, not through online checkout. At Arvada/Westminster, the Toast menu lists box lunches at $13.99 each (any regular burrito, chips and salsa and a fresh-baked chocolate chip cookie), a build-your-own burrito catering bar at $14.99 per person for groups of more than 20, and a street taco catering bar at $13.99 per person.

Box lunches have a 10-box minimum. The current catering menu asks for about 4 hours' notice on box lunches and 48 hours on catering bars, and in-house delivery is available on catering orders over $150. Call the restaurant at (303) 993-4171, call the main catering line at (720) 469-9755, or send a catering quote request with your date, headcount and address.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best breakfast burrito near Broomfield, CO?

Renegade Burrito Arvada/Westminster at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B, straight down Wadsworth Parkway from US 36 at 88th Ave, serves breakfast burritos from 7 am until close every day.

Is there a Renegade Burrito in Broomfield?

Not yet. Most of Broomfield is closest to Arvada/Westminster on Wadsworth. East Broomfield near I-25 may be closer to Westminster North at 13648 Orchard Parkway.

Is breakfast served all day?

Yes. Breakfast burritos are served from open to close at every Renegade.

What time does it open?

7 am every day. The current listing shows Mon–Thu 7 am – 8 pm · Fri–Sat 7 am – 9 pm · Sun 7 am – 8 pm.

What's the most popular breakfast burrito?

The Inferno: half bacon and half chorizo with potatoes, eggs, cheddar jack cheese, Renegade hot sauce and roasted jalapeño pico on a jalapeño cheddar tortilla.

How much is a breakfast burrito?

On the Oct 26, 2026 Toast menu, the build-your-own breakfast burrito was $8.29, T's Gunslinger $8.79, the Inferno $8.89 and the Mile High $10.79.

Can I get breakfast burritos delivered to Broomfield?

Delivery is chosen at Toast checkout and depends on your address being in range. Enter your Broomfield address on Toast to check.

Can I order breakfast for a Broomfield office?

Yes. Call (303) 993-4171 or (720) 469-9755, or send a catering quote request with your start time and headcount.
