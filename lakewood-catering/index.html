Catering in Lakewood, CO

Planning an event in Lakewood? Renegade Burrito brings bold Mexican flavors and hassle-free catering to gatherings of every size — corporate lunches, weddings, family celebrations and neighborhood events. Lakewood catering is handled by the Lakewood team.

Request a catering quoteCall to order (720) 255-2011

Catering for every occasion

  • Corporate catering — easy-to-serve burrito boxes or taco bars for offices, trainings and luncheons.
  • Wedding catering — taco and burrito bars for receptions, rehearsals and after-parties.
  • Birthdays & private parties — self-serve bars that make hosting easy.
  • Community events — box lunches and bars for schools, teams and nonprofits.

Service area: Serving Lakewood, Golden, Wheat Ridge and the Denver Federal Center area from 220 Union Blvd.

Catering contact: Email renegadeburritounion@gmail.com

Lakewood catering FAQs

How do I order catering from Lakewood?

Call (720) 255-2011 or request a quote below. Catering can't be ordered online.

What is the minimum for catering?

Box lunches start at 10 people. Catering bars are designed for 20 or more.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery fees vary by distance; delivery with setup is available.
