Office lunch, handled

Office lunch catering in metro Denver: boxed burrito lunches from 10 people, catering bars for 20 or more.

Order catering onlineRequest a quote

Office lunches have two jobs: show up on time and make everyone happy. A Renegade box lunch covers both — a regular-size burrito, chips and salsa and a fresh-baked chocolate chip cookie in one box per person.

For a bigger team lunch, a burrito catering bar lets everyone build their own with tortillas, rice, beans, proteins, cheese, lettuce, sour cream and salsas.

Recurring lunch? Order from the same location each time and earn Burrito Bucks on the orders you place on Toast.

Best-fit packages

  • Box lunches — Regular burrito, chips & salsa and a fresh-baked chocolate chip cookie, boxed per person
  • Burrito catering bar — Build-your-own bar: tortillas, rice, beans, proteins, cheese, lettuce, sour cream and salsas

Good to know

  • Minimum 10 box lunches.
  • 4 hours' notice preferred for box lunches; 48 hours for catering bars.
  • Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and dairy-free needs can often be accommodated.
