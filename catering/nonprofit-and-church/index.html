Nonprofit and church event catering

Catering for church gatherings, volunteer days and nonprofit events across metro Denver, plus Fundraising Days.

Request a catering quoteCall to order (720) 469-9755

Volunteer days, church potlucks, youth group nights and nonprofit galas all need a meal that's easy to serve. Burrito and street taco catering bars are self-serve, and box lunches are labeled one per person.

Raising money instead? Renegade Fundraising Days let schools and nonprofits earn a share of sales from a single day at a participating location. See the Fundraising page for how to apply.

Tell us about your budget and headcount in the quote form, and the location team will recommend the right mix.

Best-fit packages

  • Box lunches — Regular burrito, chips & salsa and a fresh-baked chocolate chip cookie, boxed per person
  • Burrito catering bar — Build-your-own bar: tortillas, rice, beans, proteins, cheese, lettuce, sour cream and salsas
  • Street taco catering bar — 4 white corn tortillas per person, proteins, cotija, cilantro, onions, lime, salsas, beans and rice

Good to know

  • Box lunches start at 10.
  • Catering bars feed 20 or more.
  • Fundraising Days are approved by each location.
