Graduation party catering

Graduation party catering in metro Denver: street taco bars, burrito bars and box lunches for open houses of 20 to 1,000.

Order catering onlineRequest a quote

Grad parties are open houses: guests come and go for hours. Self-serve catering bars keep food ready without anyone stuck behind a grill.

Street taco bars with corn tortillas, proteins, cotija, cilantro, onions and lime are a grad-season favorite. Burrito bars work well for bigger crowds.

Book early — May and June weekends fill up. Request a quote with your date and headcount and the location team will confirm.

Best-fit packages

  • Burrito catering bar — Build-your-own bar: tortillas, rice, beans, proteins, cheese, lettuce, sour cream and salsas
  • Street taco catering bar — 4 white corn tortillas per person, proteins, cotija, cilantro, onions, lime, salsas, beans and rice

Good to know

  • Order 48 hours ahead for catering bars.
  • Ask about delivery with setup.
  • Guest count estimates can be adjusted before the event.
