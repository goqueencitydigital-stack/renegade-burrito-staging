Breakfast burritos in Lakewood

Breakfast burritos all day on Union Blvd in Lakewood, on the way to the foothills. Order pickup or delivery on Toast.

Order onlineLakewood location

Renegade Burrito Lakewood at 220 Union Blvd is our west-side kitchen, close to 6th Avenue and on the way toward Golden and the foothills.

Pick up a breakfast burrito before a hike or a show at Red Rocks: The Inferno, The Mile High, T's Gunslinger or your own build.

Lakewood's catering menu includes box lunches, catering bars and street taco bars. Email the Lakewood team or request a quote.
