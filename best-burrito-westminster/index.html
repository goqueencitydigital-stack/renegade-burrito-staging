Where to get a great burrito in Westminster

If you live or work in Westminster and want a burrito that's worth the drive, head to Renegade Burrito Arvada/Westminster at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B. The restaurant sits on Wadsworth Blvd at 88th Ave, right on the line between Arvada and Westminster, so a lot of Westminster regulars think of it as their neighborhood Renegade.

Renegade Burrito started in Westminster in 2017. Today there are six Renegade restaurants across metro Denver, and the Wadsworth store is the one with the patio, the margaritas and happy hour every day from 2 to 5 pm. You can dine in, sit outside, pick up or order delivery on Toast.

Burritos are made to order on a freshly steamed tortilla. Pick a Customer Favorite, like Lenny's Outlaw with steak guisado or the Desperado with pork chili verde and carnitas, or build your own with your protein, rice or seasoned potatoes, beans and salsa.

The burritos Westminster orders most

Renegade calls its signature builds Customer Favorites. Each one has a set combination of protein, filling, cheese, salsa and tortilla, and most come on Renegade's jalapeño cheddar tortilla. These are the Customer Favorites on the Arvada/Westminster Toast menu right now, with the price listed there.

If you're new to Renegade, two good starting points are the Double R (Renegade chicken, potatoes, mild pico and tomatillo ranch) for something easy-going, and Lenny's Outlaw (steak guisado, rice, pinto beans, mango pico and Renegade hot sauce) when you want more heat and a bigger meal.

What makes a Renegade burrito different

The tortilla

Most Customer Favorites are wrapped in a jalapeño cheddar tortilla instead of plain flour. You can also choose a flour tortilla, and street tacos are served on grilled white corn tortillas.

The proteins

The protein lineup includes Renegade chicken, chipotle grilled chicken, steak guisado, carnitas, pork chili verde, bacon, chorizo and sausage. Pork chili verde shows up in several favorites, including Brad's Renegade and Big Al's Bonanza.

Potatoes, not just rice

A lot of Renegade burritos use seasoned potatoes in place of rice, which gives them a breakfast-burrito heartiness at any time of day. The seasoned potatoes are popular enough to have their own menu item, the World Famous Potato.

House salsas and sauces

The salsa bar is where Renegade gets its personality: pico, roasted jalapeño pico, mango pico, strawberry habanero, tomatillo ranch, salsa verde, roja sauce and Renegade hot sauce. See every salsa and sauce.

Build your own burrito, your way

Prefer to make your own? The build-your-own burrito at Arvada/Westminster is listed at $8.29 on Toast. It starts with a freshly steamed tortilla and melted cheddar or jack cheese, then you choose:

  1. Protein: Renegade chicken, chipotle grilled chicken, steak guisado, carnitas, pork chili verde, bacon, chorizo or sausage.
  2. Filling: rice, beans or seasoned potatoes.
  3. Beans: pinto or black.
  4. Fresh toppings: lettuce, onions, cilantro, sour cream and guacamole.
  5. Salsa: anything from mild pico to strawberry habanero.

Want it without the tortilla? The bowl uses the same ingredients at the same listed price, and the quesadilla is grilled with the same choices. Options and upcharges are always current on Toast, so final pricing shows at checkout. Build-your-own menu.

Patio, margaritas and happy hour

The Arvada/Westminster store is the only Renegade that pours margaritas and beer, and it runs happy hour every day from 2 to 5 pm. That makes it an easy after-work stop if you're coming from Westminster on 88th or 92nd Ave. See happy hour details.

The patio can host private events for up to 40 guests, with no reservation fee, just a minimum spend that depends on the day and time. It works for team lunches, birthday dinners and watch parties. Plan a patio event.

Getting here from around Westminster

The restaurant is on Wadsworth Blvd at 88th Ave. Wadsworth is a main north–south road through the area, so it's easy to reach from most of west Westminster.

  • From Westminster City Hall (4800 W 92nd Ave): head west toward Wadsworth, then south to 88th Ave.
  • From Standley Lake: the park sits on Westminster's western edge, bordered by 86th Parkway on the south and Kipling St on the east. The restaurant is about 3 miles east of the main park entrance at 100th and Simms in a straight line.
  • From US 36: take the Wadsworth Parkway exit and go south on Wadsworth to 88th Ave.

Use the directions button for turn-by-turn directions from where you are. The site never asks for your location unless you tap "Use my location."

Which Westminster Renegade is which?

Renegade has two stores with "Westminster" in the name, and it's easy to pick the wrong one when you order online. Here's the quick rule:

  • Arvada/Westminster is at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B on Wadsworth Blvd at 88th Ave. It has the patio, daily happy hour and margaritas. This is the store this page is about.
  • Westminster North is at 13648 Orchard Parkway, at 136th Ave and I-25 by Orchard Town Center, about 7.8 miles away in a straight line. Westminster North details.

When you check out on Toast, look for the Wadsworth Blvd address at the top of the order before you pay.

Feeding a crowd in Westminster

Renegade catering is ordered by phone or with a quote request, not through online checkout. At Arvada/Westminster, the Toast menu lists box lunches at $13.99 each (any regular burrito, chips and salsa and a fresh-baked chocolate chip cookie), a build-your-own burrito catering bar at $14.99 per person for groups of more than 20, and a street taco catering bar at $13.99 per person.

Box lunches have a 10-box minimum. The current catering menu asks for about 4 hours' notice on box lunches and 48 hours on catering bars, and in-house delivery is available on catering orders over $150. Call the restaurant at (303) 993-4171, call the main catering line at (720) 469-9755, or send a catering quote request with your date, headcount and address.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best burrito in Westminster, CO?

Renegade Burrito Arvada/Westminster at 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B, on Wadsworth Blvd at 88th Ave on the Arvada–Westminster border, serves Customer Favorite burritos, build-your-own burritos and house salsas.

Is Renegade Burrito Arvada/Westminster in Arvada or Westminster?

The mailing address is 8770 Wadsworth Blvd, Unit B, Arvada, CO 80003. The store sits on Wadsworth Blvd right on the Arvada–Westminster border, which is why it's called Arvada/Westminster.

What are Renegade's most popular burritos?

Renegade's signature builds are called Customer Favorites. At Arvada/Westminster they include Lenny's Outlaw, the Desperado, Lola G's Bandit, Big Al's Bonanza, Brad's Renegade, the Double R and the Spacebird.

How much is a burrito at Renegade Arvada/Westminster?

On the Oct 26, 2026 Toast menu, the build-your-own burrito was $8.29 and Customer Favorites ranged from $9.99 to $14.29. Toast shows the current price at checkout.

Can I order online for pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order on Renegade's Toast page for the Arvada/Westminster store and choose pickup or delivery at checkout. Delivery depends on your address being in range.

What are the hours?

The current listing shows Mon–Thu 7 am – 8 pm · Fri–Sat 7 am – 9 pm · Sun 7 am – 8 pm. Hours can change on holidays, so check Toast before you go.

Does it have a patio and happy hour?

Yes. Arvada/Westminster has a patio and runs happy hour every day from 2 to 5 pm. It's the only Renegade that serves margaritas and beer.

Is this the same as the Westminster North store?

No. Westminster North is a separate Renegade at 13648 Orchard Parkway, near 136th Ave and I-25. Check the address on Toast before you pay so your order goes to the right store.

Does Renegade cater in Westminster?

Yes. Catering is ordered by phone or quote request. Call (303) 993-4171 or the catering line at (720) 469-9755, or send a quote request online.
