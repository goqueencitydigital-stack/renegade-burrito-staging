Catering Bar Setup & Delivery

Catering Bar Setup & Delivery for Renegade Burrito catering orders.

Order catering onlineRequest a quote

Catering delivery fees vary by distance, and in-house delivery is available on catering orders over $150. Delivery with setup includes warming racks, sternos and disposable pans.

The fee for your order is shown on Toast at checkout or confirmed by the location with your quote.
