Roles at Renegade

  • Team members — Build burritos, run the line, take care of guests.
  • Prep cooks — Make our salsas, proteins and green chili from scratch.
  • Shift leads — Run a shift, coach the crew, keep quality high.
  • Management — Lead a restaurant's people, food cost and guest experience.
  • Catering — Prep, pack and deliver big orders on time.

Open positions vary by location. Listing a role here doesn't mean every restaurant is hiring for it today.
